What is a Forensic Audit?

A forensic audit is a specialised examination of an organisation's financial records conducted with the intent of gathering evidence suitable for use in legal proceedings. Unlike a standard statutory audit, forensic auditing goes beyond verifying numbers — it investigates the why and how behind financial anomalies, tracing the trail of fraud, corruption, or financial misconduct.

Our forensic audit team combines deep accounting expertise with investigative skills to uncover hidden liabilities, inflated expenses, diverted funds, and manipulated books — delivering findings that are factually accurate, legally sound, and court-admissible.

Red Flags That Demand a Forensic Audit

These warning signs often indicate deeper financial irregularities that require immediate investigation.

📉

Unexplained Revenue Decline

Sudden drops in revenue or margins without a clear business reason may indicate diversion of sales.

🧾

Suspicious Vendor Payments

Payments to unfamiliar vendors, duplicate invoices, or round-number transactions without documentation.

👤

Employee Living Beyond Means

Employees with access to funds displaying lifestyles inconsistent with their reported compensation.

🔐

Override of Internal Controls

Management frequently bypassing approval processes, access controls, or segregation of duties.

📂

Missing or Altered Records

Gaps in documentation, altered entries, or records that cannot be reconciled with bank statements.

💸

Unexplained Cash Outflows

Large or frequent cash withdrawals, transfers to related parties, or unrecorded liabilities.
