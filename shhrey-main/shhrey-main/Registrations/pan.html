Permanent Account Number (PAN)

Unique tax identifier for individuals and businesses. Essential for income tax filing and financial transactions.

What is PAN?

Permanent Account Number (PAN) is a unique 10-character alphanumeric identifier issued by the Indian Income Tax Department. It is mandatory for all individuals and entities earning income in India or conducting financial transactions exceeding specified thresholds.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Unique identifier for tax purposes
  • Required for income tax return filing
  • Mandatory for opening bank accounts
  • Essential for property transactions
  • Needed for credit card applications
  • Required for foreign investment
  • Lifetime validity - never expires
  • Single PAN for all bank accounts
  • Proof of income eligibility
  • Facilitates loan and credit access

Who Needs PAN?

All individuals earning income, business owners, professionals, freelancers, companies, partnerships, trusts, and any entity conducting financial transactions above specified limits must obtain PAN.

Requirements for PAN Registration

For Individuals:

  • Aadhaar number or passport
  • Date of birth proof
  • Address proof (utility bill, rental agreement)
  • Valid photo ID

For Business Entities:

  • Certificate of Incorporation (for companies)
  • Partnership Deed (for partnerships)
  • Business registration documents
  • Authorized signatory details
  • Business address proof

PAN Application Process - Step by Step

  1. Visit ITD Website: Go to income-tax.gov.in
  2. Select Application Form: Choose Form 49A (Individual) or Form 49AA (Foreigner)
  3. Fill Online Form: Provide personal/business details
  4. Upload Documents: Attach proof of identity and address
  5. Submit Application: Submit form with service fee
  6. Receive Acknowledgment: Get reference number and receipt
  7. Track Status: Monitor application status online
  8. Receive PAN: Get PAN via email/post (1-2 weeks)

Documents Required

Proof of Identity (Any one):

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport
  • Voter ID
  • Driving License
  • PF Account Statement

Proof of Address (Any one):

  • Utility Bills (Electricity, Water, Telephone)
  • Rental Agreement
  • Bank Statement
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport
  • Property Registration

Proof of Date of Birth:

  • Birth Certificate
  • Passport
  • High School Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card

PAN and Aadhaar Linking

It is now mandatory to link your Aadhaar with PAN. This is done automatically during PAN application if Aadhaar is provided. You can also link existing PAN with Aadhaar through:

  • Income Tax e-filing portal
  • ITD SMS service
  • Bank/Post Office assistance

PAN Correction & Changes

If information in your PAN is incorrect, you can file an application to correct it using:

  • Form 61 (for individual applicants)
  • Form 61A (for corporate applicants)
  • Online correction through e-filing
  • Process takes 1-2 weeks

PAN for Different Categories

Individual PAN:

For salaried employees, self-employed professionals, freelancers, and business owners earning income.

Company PAN:

For registered companies and corporate entities conducting business.

Partnership PAN:

For partnership firms registered under Partnership Act.

LLP PAN:

For Limited Liability Partnerships with multiple partners.

Trust/Society PAN:

For non-profit organizations, trusts, and societies.

Advantages of PAN

  • Single identifier for all financial transactions
  • Easier income tax compliance
  • Faster loan approvals
  • Better credit history tracking
  • Proof of income for various purposes
  • Protection against identity theft
  • Access to government schemes
  • International financial credibility

Important Notes

  • PAN is mandatory for ITR filing if income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh/year
  • Penalty for not filing ITR with PAN: Up to ₹10,000
  • Single PAN is recommended even if multiple sources of income
  • PAN should be quoted in all tax-related documents
  • Keep PAN details secure and confidential
