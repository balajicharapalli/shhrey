Subsidiary Company in India

Establish a separate legal entity in India owned by a foreign parent company.

Overview

A Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS) is an Indian company where 100% shares are held by a foreign parent entity. It is the most preferred route for multinational corporations seeking to establish a strong, independent, and fully controlled presence in India.

The subsidiary operates as an independent legal entity under Indian law — filing its own taxes, entering contracts, and holding assets — while remaining strategically directed by the overseas parent company.

Key Features

🏛️

Separate Legal Entity

Operates independently under Indian law with its own PAN, accounts, and contracts.

🛡️

Limited Liability

Parent company's liability is limited to its shareholding in the subsidiary.

🌐

100% FDI Allowed

Foreign Direct Investment permitted in most sectors under the automatic route.

🎯

Full Operational Control

Parent retains complete strategic and operational authority over the entity.

♾️

Perpetual Succession

Continues to exist regardless of changes in the parent company's ownership.

Best Suited For

Best suited for multinational corporations planning long-term business operations in India.
