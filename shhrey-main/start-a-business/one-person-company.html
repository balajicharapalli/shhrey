One Person Company (OPC)

A single-owner company structure with limited liability and full corporate recognition — the perfect launchpad for solo entrepreneurs.

What is an OPC?

A One Person Company (OPC) is a unique type of private limited company introduced under the Companies Act, 2013 that allows a single individual to incorporate and run a company with full limited liability protection.

Before OPC was introduced, a sole proprietor had unlimited personal liability for business debts. OPC bridges this gap — giving solo entrepreneurs the credibility of a registered company and the safety of limited liability, without needing a second shareholder or director.

It is governed by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and requires a nominee director who steps in only if the sole member becomes incapacitated.

Key Features of an OPC
  • Only one member (shareholder) required — a single natural person
  • Member must be an Indian citizen and resident in India
  • Separate legal entity distinct from its owner
  • Limited liability — personal assets fully protected
  • Nominee director mandatory (takes over in case of member's incapacity)
  • Perpetual succession — company survives independently
  • No minimum paid-up capital requirement
  • Can convert to Private Limited Company once turnover exceeds ₹2 crore
Benefits of Choosing OPC
  • Complete ownership and decision-making authority with one person
  • Limited liability protects personal savings, home, and assets
  • Corporate credibility — clients, banks, and vendors take you more seriously
  • Easier access to business loans and funding compared to sole proprietorship
  • Perpetual existence — business is not affected by the owner's personal events
  • Clear path to scale up by converting to a Private Limited Company
  • Eligible for government startup schemes and MSME benefits

Best Suited For

Freelancers, consultants, solo professionals, first-time entrepreneurs, and small business owners who want limited liability and corporate recognition without needing a business partner.

Documents Required
For the Member (Owner)
  • PAN Card (mandatory)
  • Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID (identity proof)
  • Latest bank statement or utility bill (address proof)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email ID and mobile number
For the Nominee Director
  • PAN Card
  • Identity and address proof
  • Written consent in Form INC-3
For Registered Office
  • Utility bill not older than 2 months
  • NOC from property owner (if rented)
  • Rental agreement (if applicable)
OPC Registration Process
1

Obtain DSC & DIN

Get Digital Signature Certificate and Director Identification Number for the member.

2

Name Approval

File SPICe+ Part A on MCA portal to reserve a unique company name.

3

Draft MOA & AOA

Prepare Memorandum and Articles of Association for the OPC.

4

File SPICe+ Part B

Submit incorporation form with all documents to MCA for registration.

5

Certificate of Incorporation

Receive COI with CIN from Registrar of Companies — company is now live.

6

PAN, TAN & Bank

Apply for PAN, TAN, and open a current account in the company's name.

Important Considerations

  • Only an Indian citizen and resident (staying in India for 182+ days in preceding year) can form an OPC
  • One person cannot incorporate more than one OPC
  • OPC cannot carry out Non-Banking Financial Investment activities
  • Mandatory conversion to Private Limited if paid-up capital exceeds ₹50 lakhs or turnover exceeds ₹2 crore
  • Annual filings with MCA, income tax return, and statutory audit are mandatory

Ongoing Compliance
  • Annual Return (MGT-7A) — Filed with ROC every year
  • Financial Statements (AOC-4) — Filed annually with MCA
  • Statutory Audit — Mandatory every financial year regardless of turnover
  • Income Tax Return — Annual ITR filing compulsory
  • Board Meeting — Minimum one board meeting per half year
  • GST Returns — Monthly/quarterly if GST registered
