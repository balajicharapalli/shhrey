Business Valuation

Accurate and credible valuation of your business for mergers, acquisitions, fundraising, or regulatory requirements.

What is Business Valuation?

Business valuation is the process of determining the economic worth of a business or business unit. It is a critical exercise for business owners, investors, and stakeholders who need a reliable, defensible figure for strategic decisions.

At Shreyas U M & Associates, we apply industry-standard methodologies to deliver valuations that are accurate, credible, and accepted by regulators, investors, and courts. Whether you are planning a merger, raising capital, or meeting compliance requirements, our team ensures your valuation stands up to scrutiny.

📊 Why Valuation Matters

  • Establishes a fair and defensible price for business transactions
  • Required by regulators such as SEBI, RBI, and the Income Tax Department
  • Helps attract investors by demonstrating business worth with credible data
  • Essential for resolving partner disputes and legal proceedings
  • Aids in informed decision-making for mergers, acquisitions, and exits

Our Valuation Approaches

We apply the most appropriate methodology based on your business type, industry, and purpose of valuation. Our approach is always transparent, well-documented, and compliant with applicable standards.

Income Approach (DCF)

Values the business based on projected future cash flows, discounted to present value. Best for established, cash-generating businesses.

Most Common

Market Approach

Compares your business to similar companies that have been sold or are publicly listed to determine a fair market value.

Benchmarking

Asset-Based Approach

Values the business by summing up the fair market value of all assets and subtracting liabilities. Suitable for asset-heavy businesses.

Asset-Heavy

NAV Method

Calculates the net asset value of the business, widely used for investment companies, funds, and real estate entities.

Funds & RE

Startup Valuation

Specialised methods for early-stage companies including Scorecard, Berkus, and Venture Capital methods for pre-revenue startups.

Startups

When Do You Need a Valuation?

  • Mergers, acquisitions, and business sales
  • Fundraising from angel investors or venture capital
  • ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plan) implementation
  • Regulatory filings with SEBI, RBI, or Income Tax authorities
  • Partner buy-outs, disputes, or restructuring
  • Goodwill assessment and intangible asset valuation
  • Loan collateral and banking requirements
  • Estate planning and succession
