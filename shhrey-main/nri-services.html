Comprehensive Tax, FEMA & Investment Advisory Services for Non-Resident Indians

Who is an NRI?

A Non-Resident Indian (NRI) is an Indian citizen residing outside India for more than 182 days in a financial year. Managing taxation and financial matters in India while staying abroad requires professional guidance and compliance support.

From income tax filing and FEMA compliance to property advisory and repatriation, navigating Indian financial regulations from overseas can be complex. We simplify it for you with expert, personalised guidance.
