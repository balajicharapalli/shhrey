About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Shreyas U M & Associates ("we," "our," or "the Firm") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information that you provide when you visit our website, contact us, or engage us for professional services including audit, taxation, advisory, compliance, business registration, and related financial services.

By using our website or engaging our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our website and services.

This policy applies to all clients, prospective clients, website visitors, and third parties who interact with us in connection with our services.

Website & Communication Information

  • Name and email address submitted through contact or enquiry forms
  • IP address, browser type, and pages visited on our website
  • Correspondence via email, phone calls, and in-person meetings

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the purpose of delivering our professional services and fulfilling legal obligations. We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your engagement with us.

  • Preparation and filing of income tax returns, GST returns, and other statutory filings
  • Conducting statutory audits, internal audits, and forensic examinations
  • Business registration, incorporation, and compliance with MCA, ROC, and SEBI requirements
  • Business valuation, due diligence, and financial advisory services
  • FEMA compliance, NRI taxation, and repatriation assistance
  • Responding to enquiries and communicating regarding your engagement
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, and professional obligations under ICAI guidelines
  • Maintaining our records as required by law and professional standards
4

Professional Confidentiality

As Chartered Accountants, we are bound by the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). This code imposes a strict duty of professional confidentiality on every member of the Firm.

ICAI Confidentiality Obligation: Under Clause 1 of Part I of the First Schedule to the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, and the ICAI Code of Ethics, every Chartered Accountant is duty-bound to maintain the confidentiality of client information acquired during professional engagements, and to not disclose such information to any third party without proper and specific authority, unless there is a legal or professional right or duty to disclose.

This professional obligation of confidentiality exists independently of this Privacy Policy and provides you with an additional layer of legal protection beyond standard data protection law. All partners, staff, and associates of this Firm are bound by this obligation.

What Confidentiality Covers

  • All financial information, documents, and records shared with us
  • Business strategies, financial positions, and internal matters
  • Legal disputes, tax assessments, and regulatory proceedings
  • Personal financial affairs and family financial matters
  • Information about your company's directors, shareholders, and operations

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to any third party. We share your information only in the following limited and necessary circumstances:

Statutory & Regulatory Authorities

  • Income Tax Department — for filing returns, responding to notices, and assessments
  • GST Council and GST Network (GSTN) — for GST registration and return filing
  • Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and Registrar of Companies (ROC)
  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) — for FEMA filings and foreign exchange matters
  • Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) — where applicable
  • Any other authority where disclosure is required by law or court order

Professional Service Providers

  • Co-auditors or joint auditors where professional standards require
  • Legal counsel, advocates, or consultants engaged to assist your matter
  • Technology providers for secure document management (under strict NDA)
Note: Any sharing with professional service providers is done only with your knowledge and under binding confidentiality agreements. We never share your information for commercial, advertising, or marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, and as required by applicable law and professional standards.

Type of Record Retention Period Basis
Income tax documents & returns 8 years from relevant assessment year Income Tax Act, 1961
GST records and returns 6 years from due date of annual return CGST Act, 2017
Audit working papers 7 years from date of audit report ICAI Standards on Auditing
Company registration documents Duration of engagement + 8 years Companies Act, 2013
General client correspondence 5 years after end of engagement Limitation Act, 1963
Website enquiry forms 2 years if no engagement results Internal policy

After the applicable retention period, records are securely deleted or destroyed in a manner that prevents reconstruction or unauthorised access.

7

Data Security

We take the security of your personal and financial information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

  • All digital documents are stored on password-protected, encrypted systems
  • Access to client files is restricted to authorised personnel involved in your engagement
  • Physical documents are stored in locked cabinets with restricted access
  • Secure email and communication channels are used for sensitive correspondence
  • Staff are trained on data confidentiality obligations and ICAI ethical standards
  • Regular security reviews of our systems and access controls
Please Note: While we implement robust security measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to communicate sensitive financial information through secure, verified channels only. If you suspect any breach, please contact us immediately.
8

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and our professional obligations, you have the following rights with respect to your personal information held by us:

  • Right to Access — You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction — You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Withdrawal of Consent — Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time (note: this will not affect lawful processing carried out prior to withdrawal).
  • Right to Data Portability — You may request your data in a structured, commonly used format where technically feasible.
  • Right to Erasure — You may request deletion of your data, subject to our legal and professional retention obligations.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal — You may raise a complaint or grievance regarding our handling of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us in writing at info@shreyasum.com. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

Important: Certain rights may be limited where we are required by law to retain or process your information — for example, where mandatory regulatory filings have already been made, or where records are required to be maintained under the Income Tax Act, Companies Act, or ICAI standards.

Cookies & Website Usage

Our website uses minimal cookies to ensure proper functioning and to understand how visitors use the site. We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking, or profiling purposes.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies — Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies — Help us understand how visitors navigate the site (e.g., pages visited, time spent). Data is aggregated and anonymised.

You may disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain functionality of the website.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or social media cookies on our website.
